---
title: Content Guidelines
description: Standards for writing events, briefings, decisions, and scenario material.
---

## Tone

Write like a high-quality strategic briefing. The product should feel grounded, specific, and historically literate. Avoid meme phrasing, omniscient certainty, and empty drama.

## Events

Events need enough context to be playable:

- Who is involved.
- Why it matters now.
- What changed on the ground.
- What choices are available.

## Decisions

Decision options should be meaningfully different. Avoid three labels that all imply the same outcome. A good decision trades speed against legitimacy, security against cost, or ideology against coalition management.

## Alternate History

Alternate history should remain legible. When the timeline diverges, preserve causal chains so players can understand why the world changed.

## Sensitive Topics

Historical strategy touches war, repression, imperialism, famine, and civil conflict. Handle these topics plainly and seriously. Do not turn harm into spectacle.
